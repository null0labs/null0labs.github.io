About Me

Engineer by day Link to heading

Hey, I’m Matt a Senior Network Engineer with 20+ years logged on the command line, architecting, securing, and scaling highly avaliable infrastructure across enterprise-grade networks. Null0 Labs is where I config, break, fix, and iterate. Sharing what I know, documenting what I learn, and deep-diving into everything from routing, switching and firewalls to multi-vendor lab setups. I thrive on learning something new each day and converting that into clean configs, clear content, and a few laughs along the way.

On my channel, you’ll find hands-on walkthroughs and simulations covering:

  • 🚀 Routing & Switching fundamentals and advanced configs
  • 🛡️ Firewalls & security with Fortinet, Palo Alto, and more
  • 🧠 Protocol mastery in Cisco, Juniper, and hybrid infrastructures
  • 🐧 A dash of Linux for tooling, automation, and terminal fluency
  • 📡 Streaming live topologies and lab breakdowns

If you’re into building, breaking, and bettering your network skills—Null0 Labs is your new home.
